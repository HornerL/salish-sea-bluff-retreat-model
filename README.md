## Salish Sea Coastal Bluff Retreat Model

This model predicts **coastal bluff erosion rates in the Salish Sea and Puget Sound** in response to historical conditions and changing sea levels.

## Model Description

The model is described in the following manuscript, currently in review:

> Grossman, E., Horner, L., Vitousek, S., Chiasson, K., Little, C., and MacLennan, A., 2026, *Model of coastal bluff retreat accounting for complex geology of the Salish Sea*, **Scientific Data**, In review. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-10884802/v1.

## Model Results

The model results are published here as a USGS Data release:

> Grossman, E.E., Horner, L., Foxgrover, A.C., and O’Neill, A.C., 2026, *Projections of coastal bluff retreat in Puget Sound*, **U.S. Geological Survey data release**, https://doi.org/10.5066/P13GUZTZ.

## Data Sources

### Digital Elevation Models

Digital Elevation Models (DEMs) were sourced from the [USGS Coastal National Elevation Database (CoNED)](https://www.usgs.gov/coastal-changes-and-impacts/coned).

### Model Boundary Conditions

Wave and water level data used in this model were sourced from the [USGS Coastal Storm Modeling System (CoSMoS): Salish Sea](https://cmgds.marine.usgs.gov/data-releases/community/Cosmos_SalishSea/).

Other model boundary conditions are available in the model results [USGS Data Release](https://cmgds.marine.usgs.gov/data-releases/datarelease/10.5066-P13GUZTZ/).
